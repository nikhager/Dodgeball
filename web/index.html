Dodgeball

My 2014 Meggy Jr RGB game, running in the browser. The original Arduino sketch is compiled unchanged to WebAssembly against a stand-in for the Meggy Jr library.

How to play

You are red, on the left half of the court. Throw at the green opponents on the right and dodge their yellow balls. The row of lights above the screen shows the stage, one through seven, then the boss.

About the emulation

Colors are converted from the handheld's LED brightness levels, so they approximate the real screen. Opponents move in the same pattern every power-on, as they did on the hardware, because the sketch never seeds its random numbers.
